I enjoyed bike-packing in California and Europe but didn't plan a trip near DC
in my first five years living here.
Small day trips introduced me to rides in the area and I kept hearing about the C&O Canal Trail.
I finally gave it a try in July 2026 using Amtrak to make it a one-way, two-day ride from
Harper's Ferry to DC with plenty of time for historical sites, swimming in the river, good food,
microbreweries, and milk shakes.
35 miles/day is a comfortable pace for enjoying stops along the way.
I can't wait to to it again and start further up the river next time.
